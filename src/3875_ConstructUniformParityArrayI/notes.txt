This was really funny.

So I knew that 

even_number + even_number = even_number

So thought, what about subtraction?

this makes sense
even_n - even_n = even_n 

what about
even_n - odd_n = ?
well, odd_n is just even_n+1, so you'll get a odd_n

it made sense, once I imagined this case
[2, 4, 6, 7]
it can't be made into an all even list, because
odd - even = odd.

but it can be made into an all odd because
even - odd = odd.

so either the list
[all even] ; which is true
[all odd]  ; which is true

[mix of even and odd], and in that case,

They could be turned into all even or all odd.

This is why it is so important, to first analyze the problem than to
touch the keyboard.
